Systems

Different crops require different protection systems.

Bird netting, hail netting, insect exclusion and weather protection all behave differently. Cover type, mesh, support spacing and seasonal operation need to match the crop and region.

Bird Netting

Weather Protection

Commercial orchard focus

Protection systems designed for real orchard operation.

Orchard protection should not create more problems than it solves. Arcovra’s focus is practical layouts, manageable installation and systems that can realistically operate during harvest seasons.



The long-term goal is to develop systems that reduce labour intensity, improve seasonal handling and better suit large-scale Australian orchard layouts.

Pilot installations

Early-stage pilot systems.

Arcovra is currently focused on pilot orchard installations and system development. The objective is to test practical installation methods, seasonal operation and long-term durability before wider rollout.

Pilot projects are intended as collaborative grower developments. Each installation helps refine spacing, structures, anchoring systems, net operation and orchard integration.


The focus is proof through testing and real orchard performance rather than overpromising early commercial claims.
